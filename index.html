About

Hi, I'm Kanchan.

I'm a PhD student in the Department of Data Science and Engineering at IISER Bhopal, supervised by Prof. Vaibhav Kumar. My research focuses mainly on developing "A Data-Driven Decision-Making Toolbox for Urban Redevelopment," with a primary focus on improving urban quality of life. I hold an M.Sc. in Mathematics from IIT Kanpur and a B.Sc. (Hons.) in Mathematics from Raj Rishi Autonomous College, Alwar, Raj. Outside research, I play badminton, read, write, and travel.

Research

What I work on

I work on urban planning research, focusing on understanding the factors that shape quality of life in cities. My work is to develop data-driven methods to identify what's driving poor outcomes in specific urban areas, and to figure out what kinds of interventions could realistically improve them. The broader goal is to make urban redevelopment more evidence-based, so that cities can prioritize changes that meaningfully improve everyday life for residents.
